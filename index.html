About

Teacher and performer.

Anastasia has over sixteen years of experience teaching students at all levels. She holds a Master’s degree in Violin and Viola Performance, having graduated from leading universities in both the UK and Russia.

Alongside teaching, she performs with some of the leading orchestras in London and Oxford, combining her extensive performance background with a dedication to nurturing the next generation of musicians.
